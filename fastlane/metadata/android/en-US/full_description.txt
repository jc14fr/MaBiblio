Ma Biblio lets you catalogue your books, comics, DVDs, games, tools and other objects. Organise them in categories and subcategories, and track loans and borrowings with the people involved.

Features:
- Collection organised by categories and subcategories
- Loans and borrowings, with the people concerned
- Wishlist
- Search in your collection
- Look up books by ISBN (optional, uses Open Library and BnF)
- Export and import of a backup as a ZIP file

All data is stored on your device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
